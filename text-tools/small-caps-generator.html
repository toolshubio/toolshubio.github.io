What Is a Small Caps Generator?

A small caps generator converts your lowercase letters into small capital Unicode characters. "Hello World" becomes "Hᴇʟʟᴏ Wᴏʀʟᴅ" — the letters are capitals, but sized to match the height of lowercase text. Because the output uses real Unicode characters rather than a font style, it keeps its look when you copy and paste it into apps that would normally strip formatting: social bios, comments, usernames, documents, and messages.

Small caps sit in a sweet spot between lowercase and ALL CAPS. Full capitals feel like shouting and tire the eye in long stretches; small caps carry the same emphasis with a lighter touch, which is why designers have used them in print for centuries.

How to Use the Small Caps Generator

Type or paste your text into the input box. The small caps version appears instantly — most generators convert as you type. Click the copy button, then paste the result wherever you want it: a bio, a heading, a post, a message.

One practical note: a few characters have no small-cap equivalent in Unicode, and some platforms render certain glyphs slightly differently. After pasting somewhere new, glance at the result once to make sure every letter converted the way you expected.

How Small Caps Work

Your keyboard cannot type small caps directly, so the generator cheats — cleverly. It swaps each letter for a lookalike character from Unicode blocks like Phonetic Extensions (U+1D00–U+1D7F). The letter "a" becomes "ᴀ", "e" becomes "ᴇ", "o" becomes "ᴏ". These are genuinely different characters that happen to look like tiny capitals, which is why the styling survives copy-paste: there is no formatting to strip, only characters to display.

This is also why small caps work in places fonts cannot reach. A username field has no bold or italic option, but it accepts Unicode characters — so your styled text goes through untouched.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a small caps generator?

An online tool that converts your text into small capital Unicode letters (Sᴍᴀʟʟ Cᴀᴘs), which you can copy and paste anywhere plain text is accepted.

Is the small caps generator free?

Yes — free to use with no account, no downloads, and no conversion limits.

Will small caps work in my Instagram bio?

Yes. The output is Unicode text, not a font, so it displays in Instagram, TikTok, Discord, X, WhatsApp, and most other apps.

Why do some letters look unchanged?

A few characters have no small-cap equivalent in Unicode, so the generator leaves them as-is. This is a Unicode limitation, not a tool bug.

Can screen readers read small caps text?

Often poorly — screen readers may spell out unfamiliar Unicode characters. Avoid small caps for critical information like names, prices, or instructions.

Is my text uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

What is the difference between small caps and ALL CAPS?

ALL CAPS uses full-size capital letters, which reads as shouting. Small caps use compact capitals sized like lowercase letters — emphasis without the loudness.

Can I use small caps in a Word document?

You can paste generated small caps into Word, though Word also has a built-in small caps font effect (Format → Font → Small caps) that works on normal text.
