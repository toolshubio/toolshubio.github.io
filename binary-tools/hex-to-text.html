Hex to Text Converter — Free Online Hex Decoder

Packet captures, hex dumps, and firmware readouts all have one thing in common: the readable text is hiding inside, wearing a hexadecimal disguise. I have stared at enough Wireshark output to know that 48 65 6C 6C 6F looks like noise until someone tells you it spells "Hello." A hex to text converter does that telling — paste the hex, get the words back.

What Is a Hex to Text Converter?

Hexadecimal is base-16, and two hex digits make exactly one byte — the same byte that stores one ASCII character. So each pair of hex digits is one letter: 48 is 72 in decimal, which is H in ASCII. String the pairs together and a hex dump becomes a sentence. "48 65 6C 6C 6F 20 57 6F 72 6C 64" decodes to "Hello World."

The converter handles the messy reality of hex input. Continuous strings (48656C6C6F), space-separated pairs (48 65 6C 6C 6F), and 0x-prefixed values (0x48 0x65) all decode the same way, because a good tool strips everything that is not a hex digit before it starts. Uppercase and lowercase letters are treated identically — 6C and 6c both decode to l.

One thing worth knowing: modern text is usually UTF-8, which is backward compatible with ASCII for English. Accented characters and emoji appear as multi-byte sequences — é becomes C3 A9 — and a UTF-8-aware converter reassembles those sequences into the right characters instead of decoding each byte separately.

How to Use the Hex to Text Converter

Copy your hex string from the packet capture, hex editor, log file, or puzzle page. Paste it into the input box — formatting does not matter much, the tool normalizes it. Click Convert and the decoded text appears below.

Copy the result to your clipboard or download it as a .txt file. If you are debugging a network capture, I recommend decoding in small chunks around the area you care about (HTTP headers, for instance) rather than dumping megabytes at once — the signal-to-noise ratio is much better. Students checking encoding homework can decode one word at a time and compare against their hand calculations.

One habit worth building: when the output shows � or boxes, check your byte count. Hex-to-text needs complete pairs; an odd number of hex digits means a digit got lost somewhere upstream.

How Hex to Text Conversion Works

Three steps per byte — the same pipeline as binary-to-text, with a different starting point.

Step 1 — Clean and pair. All non-hex characters are stripped, then the remaining digits are split into pairs: 48656C6C6F → 48 65 6C 6C 6F.

Step 2 — Hex to decimal. Each pair converts from base-16 to decimal: 48 → 4×16+8 = 72; 65 → 6×16+5 = 101; 6C → 6×16+12 = 108.

Step 3 — Decimal to character. Each value is looked up in the ASCII/UTF-8 chart: 72 → H, 101 → e, 108 → l.

Worked example — decode "50 6C 61 6E 74": pair up → 50 6C 61 6E 74; convert → 80, 108, 97, 110, 116; look up → P, l, a, n, t. The word is "Plant." A page of hex decodes in milliseconds; the same page by hand would take the better part of an hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this hex to text converter free?

Yes — free, no account, no limits.

What format should my hex be in?

Any of them: 48656C6C6F, 48 65 6C 6C 6F, or 0x48 0x65 0x6C 0x6C 0x6F all decode to Hello.

What does 48 65 6C 6C 6F mean?

It is "Hello" — each pair is one ASCII character: 48→H, 65→e, 6C→l, 6C→l, 6F→o.

Why am I getting an error about odd digits?

Hex-to-text needs complete pairs (each byte is two digits). An odd count means a digit is missing — check for a dropped character.

Does it handle emoji and non-English text?

Yes, in UTF-8 mode. Emoji and accented characters arrive as multi-byte sequences (like C3 A9 for é) and decode correctly.

What is the difference between hex to text and hex to ASCII?

None in practice — both decode hex byte pairs through the character chart into readable text.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Decoding happens entirely in your browser — your hex never leaves your device.

How do I convert text back to hex?

Use the matching text-to-hex tool, which encodes each character as its two-digit hex pair.
